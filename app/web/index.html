Is this voice note real?

Find out which seconds sound AI-generated, before you forward it. Works in English, हिन्दी and ಕನ್ನಡ.

Check straight from WhatsApp (Android)

  1. Open this page in Chrome, open the three-dot menu and choose Install app (or Add to Home screen).

  2. In WhatsApp, long-press a voice note or video and tap Share.

  3. Pick Check Before Share. The answer opens in a few seconds.
